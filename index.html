Our Story

Essential Vending was founded by five ambitious high school students with a shared vision of revolutionizing the vending industry. We're not just building a business – we're creating our future while providing essential products and services to our community.

Our goal is ambitious but achievable: reach $100,000 in revenue with 50+ machines before we head to college. We believe in the power of innovation, hard work, and strategic thinking to transform traditional vending into something extraordinary.
